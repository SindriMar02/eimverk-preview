Barley
to cask

Four years and 163 trial distillations went into the Flóki recipe. The order below is the order it happens in, and every step is done in Iceland.

  1. Barley100% Icelandic, from our own fields
  2. WaterIcelandic spring water
  3. StillsCustom made pot stills, built in house
  4. CaskNew American oak, under Mount Hekla

Tasting and tours

A little over an hour, with a tasting of Flóki, Vor and Víti. By appointment, so book ahead.
